SwipeWise tells you which credit card to use at which store, so you earn the most cashback and points on every purchase.

Most rewards apps stop at the category — "use this card for groceries." SwipeWise goes a level deeper, down to the store and the brand. Walk into Whole Foods and it tells you to tap the card that earns 5% there, not just "a grocery card." That right-card, right-here, right-now moment is what SwipeWise is built around.

WHAT YOU GET

• Best card at every store — store- and brand-level recommendations, not generic categories.
• Arrival nudges — optional notifications surface your best card as you walk into a store, so you never have to open the app or remember a thing.
• Add your wallet in seconds — pick your cards from a catalog of hundreds of US credit cards. No forms, no card numbers.
• Every category ranked — see which of your cards wins on dining, groceries, gas, travel and more, including rotating quarterly bonuses and store-specific offers.
• Payment reminders — an optional nudge before a statement is due.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Privacy here isn't a promise, it's the architecture:

• No account. No sign-up, no email, no password — open the app and use it.
• No bank connection. SwipeWise never asks for your bank login, card numbers, or transactions, because it doesn't need them to tell you which card to use.
• On-device only. Your wallet and your rewards live in a local database on your phone.
• No telemetry. The app never phones home — not which cards you see, which notifications fire, nor what you tap.
• Your location stays yours. Your precise GPS fix never leaves the device; only a location rounded to ~110 m is sent to Google Places to find nearby stores, and no location history is kept.

OPEN SOURCE

SwipeWise is open source, so you can audit exactly how it handles your data.

Note: SwipeWise is in active development. Android today; iOS later.
