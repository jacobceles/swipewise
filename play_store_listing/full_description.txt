SwipeWise tells you which credit card to use at which store, so you can earn more cashback and points on every purchase.

Most rewards apps stop at the category: "use this card for groceries." SwipeWise goes a level deeper, down to the store and brand. Walk into Whole Foods and SwipeWise tells you which card earns the most rewards there, instead of simply recommending "a grocery card." The right card, right here, right now. That's what SwipeWise is built around.

WHAT YOU GET

• Best card at every store: get store- and brand-level recommendations instead of generic category advice.
• Arrival nudges: optional notifications surface your best card when you arrive at a store, so you don't have to open the app or remember which card to use.
• Add your wallet in seconds: choose your cards from a catalog of hundreds of US and Canadian credit cards. No forms, no card numbers.
• Every category ranked: see which of your cards earns the most on dining, groceries, gas, travel and more, including rotating quarterly bonuses and store-specific offers.
• Payment reminders: get an optional reminder before your credit card statement is due.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Privacy isn't a promise. It's the architecture:

• No account needed. No sign-up, no email, no password — open the app and use it. Signing in with Google is optional; it creates an account with your email and name, and backup is separate (off by default unless your account already has an existing backup).
• No bank connection in the free app. SwipeWise never asks for your bank login, card numbers, or transactions to recommend the right card.
• Local wallet. Your wallet and rewards data are stored on your phone; enabling backup uploads your wallet and preferences, not your transactions.
• No tracking, no ads. SwipeWise doesn't track which cards you view, which notifications fire, or what you tap. There is no advertising. Diagnostic and crash reports via Firebase Crashlytics carry an anonymous install ID and session starts with stack traces and device model — no wallet or location data is ever attached.
• Location. Nearby searches send a location rounded to ~110 m through our service to Google Places. Travel mode may send precise coordinates to the OS geocoder; updated builds first check offline US/Canada country polygons. Nearby caches and arrival diagnostics stay on your phone, not in crash reports.
• Fonts and card art. Card art may load via issuer CDNs or our catalog service and be cached; fonts load via Google Fonts.

OPEN SOURCE

SwipeWise is open source, so you can audit exactly how it handles your data.
