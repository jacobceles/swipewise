us_ca.json contains exterior rings for the US mainland, Alaska mainland and
Hawaii (Big Island), and Canada mainland, Vancouver Island and Newfoundland.
Coordinates are [longitude, latitude], rounded to 0.01 degrees; consecutive
duplicate vertices removed. Other islands/territories are intentionally not
classified offline. The caller falls back to the OS geocoder for them.

These generalized cartographic polygons are NOT precise international borders
or coastline data. Classification requires a 40 km interior margin from every
edge (including coastlines). This margin is a conservative uncertainty policy,
not a positional accuracy guarantee. Near borders/coasts, on omitted islands,
or outside these polygons, return unknown and use the OS geocoder. Never use
these polygons to infer the country of a nearby coordinate from a cached fix.
